## Notable findings

1. **Duplicate Google Business Profile listing at Fremont is splitting visibility.**
   Two separate GBP listings exist for the same Fremont address: the established
   profile (4.9★, 1,421 reviews) and a second, neglected one — "Kaplan Capital LLC
   DBA Istanbul Mediterranean Restaurant-Fremont" / "Istanbul Mediterranean
   Restaurant-2 (HALAL)" (3.0★, 2 reviews). Whichever profile Google decides to
   rank for a given query becomes "us" in this report, and the *other* one shows
   up as a self-competing entry in the same local pack (e.g. rank #2 for
   "mediterranean restaurant downtown las vegas," rank #5 for "turkish food
   downtown las vegas"). This is actively cannibalizing Fremont's local pack
   presence — likely the single biggest lever available this week.
2. **"late night food fremont street" is essentially unranked (rank 57).** The
   neglected duplicate listing (3.0★/2 reviews) is the one showing up for this
   query, while the real, well-reviewed listing isn't appearing in the local pack
   at all. This is consistent with finding #1 rather than a separate problem —
   fixing the duplicate-listing issue should recover this keyword too.
3. **Strip location is strong and stable**: rank 1 on 5 of 6 keywords, rank 2 on
   "halal food las vegas strip" behind Dave's Hot Chicken (4.4★, 915 reviews) —
   we already out-rate and out-review them, so this is winnable.
