entity name), not a real competitor. It's being reported here as a "competitor"
because the script only credits the first (rank 1) listing as "ours" per
keyword — worth a manual GBP audit to confirm and request merge/removal of the
duplicate with Google, since duplicate profiles can split reviews, confuse
customers, and dilute local-pack strength over time.
