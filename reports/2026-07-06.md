### Data-quality note (fixed this run)

The tracker initially mis-recorded a second, duplicate Google Business Profile
listing for the Fremont location ("Kaplan Capital LLC DBA Istanbul
Mediterranean Restaurant-Fremont" — 3.0★, 2 reviews) as if it were a
**competitor**. That's actually our own restaurant listed twice on Google, and
it was pulling rank #2 in several Fremont local packs behind our real listing.
The tracker script has been fixed to exclude all of our own brand's listings
from the competitor list, and it no longer appears in this snapshot's
competitor data. The duplicate listing itself is still live on Google and is
called out as an action item below.
