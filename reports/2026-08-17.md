## Notable movements

No history to compare yet — everything below is a first-look observation, not a
week-over-week change.

- **Strip is dominant everywhere except one query.** #1 local-pack rank on 5 of 6
  Strip keywords. The exception is "halal food las vegas strip" at **#2**, just
  behind Dave's Hot Chicken (4.4★, 930 reviews) — plausibly because "halal food"
  as a category pulls in higher-volume fast-casual chains that outrank a
  single-location Mediterranean/Turkish spot on review volume/velocity.
- **Fremont is #1 on 5 of 6 keywords**, but **"late night food fremont street"
  ranks #50** — effectively invisible in that local pack. Likely cause: the
  Google Business Profile's listed hours/attributes may not signal "open late,"
  or the category/description doesn't target "late night" intent the way
  competitors like Fremont Food Hall and LONGBAR do.
